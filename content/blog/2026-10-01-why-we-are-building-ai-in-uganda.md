## Building where we are

Tazon was founded on a simple belief: the best AI for Africa must be built in Africa. Not adapted from systems designed elsewhere, but developed here, with local context, languages, and needs at the centre.

Uganda's education system faces real constraints. Student-teacher ratios are high. Access to quality learning materials varies. Students preparing for UACE exams need support that understands their curriculum, their assessment format, and the competencies they are expected to demonstrate.

## What we are building

We are building Stellas AI, a curriculum-grounded learning companion for Ugandan O-Level and A-Level students. It is designed around NCDC learning outcomes and aligned to the 2026 UNEB UACE syllabus and the CBC 2025 curriculum.

We are also building Tazon Labs, where we are developing our own AI model. It is not yet released. We will share updates as we make progress.

## How we think about AI

We take responsible AI seriously. Our work is guided by UNESCO's principles on human-centred, age-appropriate AI in education. We believe AI should support learning and learner agency, not replace thinking.

We are transparent about what Stellas is and is not. It is a developing tool, not a replacement for teachers, and not yet proven to improve exam grades. Our research paper describes the rationale, design principles, and evaluation framework we are working toward.

## What comes next

We are moving toward a more rigorous evaluation of Stellas. We will validate curriculum alignment, test answer accuracy, and measure whether learners demonstrate improved understanding. We will report our findings openly, including what does not work.
